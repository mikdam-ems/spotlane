How it works

Three steps, about a minute, and no account to create.

  1. 01

    Search

    Type where you're going. See nearby car parks with walking time, price per hour and how full they are.

  2. 02

    Reserve

    Pick when you'll arrive and how long you'll stay. Add your plate and pay in JD. Your space is held for you.

  3. 03

    Drive in

    Get directions in Google Maps or Waze. At the barrier, your plate is read and the gate opens.

Questions drivers ask

How Spotlane will work once garages are live.

In this prototype, the gate, payment and free spaces are simulated so you can try the whole journey.

What if I'm running late?

Your space stays yours for 15 minutes after your arrival time. If you need longer, you can keep it for another 30 minutes from the app for a small fee.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancelling is free up to 15 minutes before your arrival time, and you get a full refund.

What if someone is parked in my bay?

Tap "Someone's parked in my bay" in the app. We'll give you the nearest free bay straight away and let the garage know.

How does the gate know it's me?

A camera at the barrier reads your number plate and matches it to your booking. If it can't, show the QR code on your pass, or call the garage from the app.

Do I need an account?

No. You only need a mobile number so we can send your pass. After your first booking you can save your details to book in one tap next time.

What do you do with my location?

We use it only to show car parks near you and to give directions. We never sell it.

Own a car park?

Fill your empty bays with drivers who have already booked. Partnerships for the Amman pilot open in early 2027.
